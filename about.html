Company introduction

A Business Shaped by Agricultural Work

Kailash Cotton Ginning Factory is an established agricultural business with a focus on cotton ginning, agricultural commodities and related products. Its company identity dates to 1975.

Our product range includes mustard oil, mustard cake and cottonseed oil cake. We approach this work with respect for the materials, attention to their handling and an understanding that dependable relationships are built through clear communication over time.

As the business continues, our purpose remains straightforward: to serve customer requirements with care and professionalism across the areas that define our work.

Established 1975

Our focus
